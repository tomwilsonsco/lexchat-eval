## 1. Question

> I am looking at the process for the reporting and publishing of Scottish Water's annual and interim reports and accounts. I am wanting to create a basic timeline of the statutory requirements of various actors including Scottish Water, the Scottish Ministers and the Auditor under the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002 and the Public Finances and Accountability (Scotland) Act 2000. I also want a more detailed note on each step and what each report and set of accounts is required to contain. Can you provide me with this information?

### 1. Summary Answer (BLUF)

There are **two tracks**, and the question asks about both.

- **Accounts.** Section 45 of the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002, with the deadline supplied by section 21(2) of the Public Finance and Accountability (Scotland) Act 2000.
- **Reports.** Section 57 of the 2002 Act, which carries the interim report to 30 September, the whole-year report, what each must contain, who gets a copy, publication, and laying before the Parliament.

An answer that reads section 45 alone covers the accounts and misses the reporting timetable entirely.

Cited to the subsection that carries each requirement:

| Requirement | Timing | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| Keep proper accounts and accounting records | continuing | 2002 Act, **s.45(1)(a)** |
| Prepare a statement of accounts for each financial year giving a true and fair view | each financial year | 2002 Act, **s.45(1)(b)** |
| Comply with Ministers' directions on content, presentation, and methods and principles | as prepared | 2002 Act, **s.45(2)(a)-(c)** |
| Send that statement to the Auditor General for Scotland for auditing | by such time as Ministers direct, and in any event **not later than 6 months** after the year end | 2002 Act, **s.45(1)(c)**; 2000 Act, **s.21(2)** |
| Audit by the Auditor General or a qualified person | after receipt | 2000 Act, **s.21(3)** |
| Auditor's report to set out findings on regularity | on reporting | 2000 Act, **s.22(1)** |
| Prepare an interim report on activities from the start of the year to **30 September** | as soon as practicable after 30 September | 2002 Act, **s.57(3)(a)** |
| Prepare a report on activities for the **whole** financial year | as soon as practicable after the year end | 2002 Act, **s.57(3)(b)** |
| Send a copy of each report to Ministers, the Commission, providers and Consumer Scotland, and **publish** it | on preparation | 2002 Act, **s.57(6)** |
| **Lay** a copy of each report before the Parliament | on receipt (Scottish Ministers) | 2002 Act, **s.57(8)** |
| **Lay** the account and audit reports before the Parliament, and publish them unless Scottish Water has already published them | **not later than 9 months** after the year end | 2000 Act, **s.22(5)** |

### 2. Detailed Analysis

#### 2.1 Section 45(1): the three duties

> **Section 45 - Accounts and audit**
> (1) Scottish Water must -
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**(a)** keep proper accounts and accounting records,
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**(b)** prepare for each financial year a statement of accounts giving a true and fair view of the state of its affairs and its income and expenditure, and
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**(c)** send the statement of accounts, **by such time as the Scottish Ministers may direct**, to the Auditor General for Scotland for auditing.

([asp/2002/3, s.45](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2002/3/section/45))

These are three distinct obligations, not one: a continuing record-keeping duty (a), an annual preparation duty with a substantive standard, "a true and fair view" (b), and a transmission duty (c).

#### 2.2 Section 45(2): what Ministers may direct about the statement

> (2) Every statement of accounts prepared by Scottish Water in accordance with this section must comply with any direction given by the Scottish Ministers relating to -
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**(a)** the information to be contained in the statement of accounts,
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**(b)** the manner in which that information is to be presented, or
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**(c)** the methods and principles according to which the statement of accounts is to be prepared.

Subsection (2) is what makes the content of the statement largely a matter of ministerial direction rather than of the Act. An answer that asks "what must the accounts contain?" and looks only at the 2002 Act will find the direction-making power, not a list.

#### 2.3 The deadline is in the 2000 Act, not the 2002 Act

Section 45(1)(c) fixes no date; it defers to ministerial direction. The backstop is general:

> **Section 21 - Audit of accounts**
> (1) This section and section 22 apply in relation to any account which is, by virtue of any enactment or prerogative instrument, required (in whatever words) to be - ... (b) **sent to the Auditor General for auditing**.
> **(2) The account must be sent to the Auditor General not later than 6 months after the end of the financial year to which the account relates.**
> (3) The account is to be audited by - (a) the Auditor General, or (b) a qualified person appearing to the Auditor General to be suitable for appointment to audit the account ...

([asp/2000/1, s.21](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2000/1/section/21))

Section 45(1)(c) is an enactment requiring an account to be sent to the Auditor General for auditing, so section 21(1)(b) is engaged and section 21(2) applies. A ministerial direction under section 45(1)(c) may set an earlier date but cannot go beyond the 6 months.

#### 2.4 What the auditor must report on

Section 22(1) requires the auditor's report to set out findings on whether the expenditure and receipts shown in the account were incurred or applied in accordance with the enactment authorising them, the Budget Act or Acts for the year, and sections 4 to 7 of the 2000 Act; and, where sums were paid out of the Scottish Consolidated Fund to meet that expenditure, whether they were applied accordingly ([asp/2000/1, s.22](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2000/1/section/22)).

This is a regularity audit sitting alongside the true and fair requirement in section 45(1)(b).

---

#### 2.5 The reporting track: section 57

Section 45 is about accounts. The reports the question asks about are in section 57, and there are two of them:

> **Section 57 - Information and reports**
> (3) Scottish Water must prepare -
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) a report on its activities during the period from the beginning of each financial year **to 30th September** in that year, and
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) a report on its activities during **the whole of each financial year**,
> as soon as practicable after the end of the period to which the report relates.
> (4) A report prepared under subsection (3)(a) **must include a statement of accounts** for the period to which the report relates; and subsection (2) of section 45 applies to such a statement of accounts as it applies to a statement of accounts prepared in accordance with that section.
> (5) A report prepared under subsection (3)(b) must include -
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) a description of the manner in which Scottish Water has complied ... with its duties under **sections 26, 28, 50 and 51**, and
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) the reasons for any failure ... to comply with its duties under sections 26 and 28.
> (6) Scottish Water must -
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) send a copy of each report prepared under subsection (3) to the Scottish Ministers, the Commission, every water services provider and sewerage services provider and Consumer Scotland, and
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) **publish** the report.
> (7) A report prepared under subsection (3) must set out - (a) any directions under section 56 given to Scottish Water during the period, and (b) the extent to which Scottish Water has complied with any requirements made under section 13(2) or 15(1) or (5) of the Water Services etc. (Scotland) Act 2005.
> (8) **The Scottish Ministers must lay a copy of a report sent to them under subsection (6) before the Parliament.**

([asp/2002/3, s.57](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2002/3/section/57))

Three points that a timeline has to carry. The **interim** report is dated to 30 September and, uniquely, must itself contain a statement of accounts, prepared to the same ministerial directions as the annual one under section 45(2). The **content** requirements for the whole-year report are express, not left to direction: compliance with sections 26, 28, 50 and 51 and the reasons for any failure under sections 26 and 28. And publication and laying are both duties here: Scottish Water publishes under section 57(6)(b), the Scottish Ministers lay under section 57(8).

#### 2.6 The last dated step: laying and publishing the account

> **Section 22 - Audit of accounts: further provisions**
> (4) The Auditor General must send to the Scottish Ministers the account, the auditor's report on the account and any report prepared by the Auditor General under subsection (3) **in sufficient time to enable the Scottish Ministers to comply with subsection (5)**.
> (5) The Scottish Ministers must -
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) **lay before the Parliament** a copy of every account and report sent to them under subsection (4), and
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) except where the account and the report are published by the body or office-holder in question, **publish** every such account and report, **not later than 9 months after the end of the financial year** to which the account relates.

([asp/2000/1, s.22](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2000/1/section/22))

This closes the accounts track: 6 months to the Auditor General under section 21(2), then 9 months to laying and publication under section 22(5). Section 22(5)(b) makes the Ministers' publication duty a fallback, biting only where Scottish Water has not published the account and report itself.

### 3. Jurisdiction & Status

- **Extent.** Both Acts extend to Scotland and were returned in revised form.
- **Publication and laying.** Section 45 itself requires only preparation and transmission to the Auditor General. The publication and laying duties sit elsewhere: section 57(6)(b) and 57(8) of the 2002 Act for the reports, and section 22(5) of the 2000 Act for the account and the audit reports.
- **Relationship to question 7.** That question asks the same shape of question about the Water Industry Commission for Scotland without naming the Public Finance and Accountability (Scotland) Act 2000. Here the user names it. The 6-month deadline in section 21(2) is the same in both.
