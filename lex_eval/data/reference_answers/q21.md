## 1. Question

> I am looking at the process for the reporting and publishing of Scottish Water's annual and interim reports and accounts. I am wanting to create a basic timeline of the statutory requirements of various actors including Scottish Water, the Scottish Ministers and the Auditor under the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002 and the Public Finances and Accountability (Scotland) Act 2000. I also want a more detailed note on each step and what each report and set of accounts is required to contain. Can you provide me with this information?

### 1. Summary Answer (BLUF)

Scottish Water's accounting duties are in **section 45 of the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002**, and the deadline for sending the accounts to the Auditor General is in **section 21(2) of the Public Finance and Accountability (Scotland) Act 2000**.

Cited to the subsection that carries each requirement:

| Requirement | Provision |
|---|---|
| Keep proper accounts and accounting records | 2002 Act, **s.45(1)(a)** |
| Prepare a statement of accounts for each financial year giving a true and fair view | 2002 Act, **s.45(1)(b)** |
| Send that statement to the Auditor General for Scotland for auditing, by such time as Ministers direct | 2002 Act, **s.45(1)(c)** |
| Send it in any event not later than 6 months after the financial year end | 2000 Act, **s.21(2)** |
| Comply with Ministers' directions on content, presentation, and methods and principles | 2002 Act, **s.45(2)(a)-(c)** |
| Audit by the Auditor General or a qualified person | 2000 Act, **s.21(3)** |
| Auditor's report to set out findings on regularity | 2000 Act, **s.22(1)** |

### 2. Detailed Analysis

#### 2.1 Section 45(1): the three duties

> **Section 45 - Accounts and audit**
> (1) Scottish Water must -
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**(a)** keep proper accounts and accounting records,
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**(b)** prepare for each financial year a statement of accounts giving a true and fair view of the state of its affairs and its income and expenditure, and
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**(c)** send the statement of accounts, **by such time as the Scottish Ministers may direct**, to the Auditor General for Scotland for auditing.

([asp/2002/3, s.45](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2002/3/section/45))

These are three distinct obligations, not one: a continuing record-keeping duty (a), an annual preparation duty with a substantive standard, "a true and fair view" (b), and a transmission duty (c).

#### 2.2 Section 45(2): what Ministers may direct about the statement

> (2) Every statement of accounts prepared by Scottish Water in accordance with this section must comply with any direction given by the Scottish Ministers relating to -
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**(a)** the information to be contained in the statement of accounts,
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**(b)** the manner in which that information is to be presented, or
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**(c)** the methods and principles according to which the statement of accounts is to be prepared.

Subsection (2) is what makes the content of the statement largely a matter of ministerial direction rather than of the Act. An answer that asks "what must the accounts contain?" and looks only at the 2002 Act will find the direction-making power, not a list.

#### 2.3 The deadline is in the 2000 Act, not the 2002 Act

Section 45(1)(c) fixes no date; it defers to ministerial direction. The backstop is general:

> **Section 21 - Audit of accounts**
> (1) This section and section 22 apply in relation to any account which is, by virtue of any enactment or prerogative instrument, required (in whatever words) to be - ... (b) **sent to the Auditor General for auditing**.
> **(2) The account must be sent to the Auditor General not later than 6 months after the end of the financial year to which the account relates.**
> (3) The account is to be audited by - (a) the Auditor General, or (b) a qualified person appearing to the Auditor General to be suitable for appointment to audit the account ...

([asp/2000/1, s.21](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2000/1/section/21))

Section 45(1)(c) is an enactment requiring an account to be sent to the Auditor General for auditing, so section 21(1)(b) is engaged and section 21(2) applies. A ministerial direction under section 45(1)(c) may set an earlier date but cannot go beyond the 6 months.

#### 2.4 What the auditor must report on

Section 22(1) requires the auditor's report to set out findings on whether the expenditure and receipts shown in the account were incurred or applied in accordance with the enactment authorising them, the Budget Act or Acts for the year, and sections 4 to 7 of the 2000 Act; and, where sums were paid out of the Scottish Consolidated Fund to meet that expenditure, whether they were applied accordingly ([asp/2000/1, s.22](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2000/1/section/22)).

This is a regularity audit sitting alongside the true and fair requirement in section 45(1)(b).

### 3. Jurisdiction & Status

- **Extent.** Both Acts extend to Scotland and were returned in revised form.
- **Publication and laying.** No provision requiring Scottish Water's statement of accounts to be **published** or **laid before the Scottish Parliament** was retrieved. Section 45 requires preparation and transmission to the Auditor General; it does not, on the text retrieved, require publication. An answer asserting a publication or laying duty for these accounts should be checked against a provision, since none was found here.
- **Relationship to question 7.** That question asks the same shape of question about the Water Industry Commission for Scotland without naming the Public Finance and Accountability (Scotland) Act 2000. Here the user names it. The 6-month deadline in section 21(2) is the same in both.
