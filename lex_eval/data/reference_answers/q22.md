## 1. Question

> is the care reform (Scotland) Act 2025 in force?

Legislation only. Case law is out of scope, so an answer that turns on a decided case is not expected to cite one here.

**Scope of this answer:** Covers whether the Care Reform (Scotland) Act 2025 is in force, distinguishing the provisions commenced by the Act itself from those left to commencement regulations. Covers whether commencement regulations have been made. Limited to legislation.

## 2. Reference answer

<!-- BEGIN REFERENCE ANSWER -->

### 1. Summary Answer (BLUF)

**Partly.** The Care Reform (Scotland) Act 2025 is **not** wholly in force, and it is **not** wholly uncommenced.

The Act received Royal Assent on **22 July 2025**. Section 39 brought **section 30 and the final Part into force on 23 July 2025**, the day after. Everything else comes into force only when the Scottish Ministers appoint a day by regulations.

The failure this question exists to catch is stopping there. Reading section 39 tells you the substantive provisions await commencement regulations. It does **not** tell you whether those regulations have since been made. **They have.** The **Care Reform (Scotland) Act 2025 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2025, SSI 2025/388**, made 4 December 2025, appointed four further commencement dates, all of which have now passed. Most of the Act's substantive provisions are in force.

An answer that reads section 39 and then volunteers that no commencement regulations have been made turns a failed search into a false negative, on a question where the user is relying on the answer to know whether a duty currently binds them.

### 2. Detailed Analysis

#### 2.1 The commencement provision

> **Section 39 - Commencement**
> (1) The following come into force on the day after Royal Assent: **section 30 and this Part**.
> (2) The other provisions of this Act come into force on such day as **the Scottish Ministers may by regulations appoint**.

([asp/2025/9, s.39](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2025/9/section/39))

The Act's own metadata records an enactment date of **22 July 2025**, so subsection (1) operated on **23 July 2025**. The Act has 56 provisions and is held in revised form with Scotland extent.

Section 39 splits the Act into two populations:

| Population | Status | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Section 30, and the Part containing section 39 (the final, general Part: [s.37 Ancillary provision](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2025/9/section/37), [s.38 Regulation-making powers](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2025/9/section/38), s.39 itself and [s.40 Short title](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2025/9/section/40)) | **In force 23 July 2025** | s.39(1) |
| All other provisions, including the substantive care reform duties | In force **only when appointed** by regulations | s.39(2) |

Subsection (2) is where the answer is decided, and SSI 2025/388 is the instrument that decides it.

Note the shape of subsection (1). It commences the machinery, the ancillary provision power and the short title, plus one substantive section. This is the ordinary pattern for a Scottish Act, and it is exactly the pattern that misleads: an Act can be "commenced" in the sense that some of it is live while none of the provisions a user cares about are.

#### 2.2 The commencement instrument: SSI 2025/388

Section 39(2) is a **pointer to a further instrument**, not an answer. The instrument is the Care Reform (Scotland) Act 2025 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2025, **SSI 2025/388 (C. 31)**, made 4 December 2025, laid 8 December 2025, in force 7 January 2026. It appoints four dates:

| Date appointed | Provisions | Authority in the instrument |
|---|---|---|
| **7 January 2026** | ss.1, 9(1) and (2), 10(1) and (2), 11, 13, 14 (all purposes except the insertion of s.78G of the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010), 16, 18, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 33, 34 | reg.2(1) and Part 1 of the schedule |
| **31 March 2026** | s.14, for all remaining purposes | reg.2(3) |
| **1 April 2026** | ss.31 and 32 | reg.2(4) and Part 2 of the schedule |
| **30 April 2026** | s.36 | reg.2(5) |

All four dates have passed, so those provisions are now in force. What is left uncommenced is everything not in that list and not commenced by section 39(1).

#### 2.3 Why this instrument is hard to reach, and what that means

**SSI 2025/388 is not in the LEX corpus.** Retrieving it by identifier returns HTTP 404, and none of the four searches run here reached it, by full title, by short title, or by subject. Its terms above are taken from the official text at [legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2025/388](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2025/388), not from the research tools.

The retrieval makes the shape of the gap concrete. The same searches readily returned commencement instruments for two other Acts of the same session:

- The Scottish Elections (Representation and Reform) Act 2025 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2025 (`ssi/2025/106`)
- The Victims, Witnesses, and Justice Reform (Scotland) Act 2025 (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2025 (`ssi/2025/393`)

So the search tool does surface commencement SSIs when it holds them. It does not hold this one. That is a **corpus gap, not a research failure**, and it is a real finding about this question: a response working only from these tools cannot reach the correct current answer. It is still the correct current answer, and the reference states it.

#### 2.4 How the answer should be framed

- **Correct.** "Partly in force. Section 30 and the final Part came into force on 23 July 2025. SSI 2025/388 then commenced most of the substantive provisions on 7 January 2026, with section 14 completed on 31 March 2026, sections 31 and 32 on 1 April 2026 and section 36 on 30 April 2026."
- **Acceptable.** The same, saying plainly which provisions could not be confirmed.
- **Wrong.** "The substantive provisions are not in force and no commencement regulations have been made."

The difference between the second and the third is one clause, and it is the whole question: **not found** is a fact about the search, **not made** is a claim about the law, and here the claim would be false.

### 3. Jurisdiction & Status

- **Extent.** Scotland. `asp/2025/9`, held in revised form, 56 provisions, extent Scotland.
- **Royal Assent.** 22 July 2025, so section 39(1) took effect 23 July 2025.
- **Currency.** Commencement is a moving target. This answer states the position as at 10 September 2026, on the basis of SSI 2025/388. A further Commencement No. 2 or later instrument may since have been made, and a correct answer says which day it speaks as at.
- **Source of the commencement detail.** SSI 2025/388 is not held by the LEX corpus, so its terms come from the official text at legislation.gov.uk. Everything about the Act itself, sections 37 to 40 included, is from the retrieval.
- **Not addressed.** Which provisions remain uncommenced is not listed exhaustively here; the instrument's schedule lists what was commenced, and everything else outside section 39(1) awaits a further instrument.
