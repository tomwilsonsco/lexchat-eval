### Summary Answer (BLUF)

Yes. Under **[section 12(1)](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2002/13/section/12) of the Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act 2002** a Scottish public
authority is not obliged to comply with a request if the projected cost of complying exceeds the
amount prescribed in regulations. The Act sets no figure itself.

The figure is set by **[regulation 5](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ssi/2004/467/regulation/5) of the Freedom of Information (Fees for Required Disclosure)
(Scotland) Regulations 2004 (SSI 2004/467)**, which prescribes **£600** for the purposes of section
12(1).

The cost is worked out under **[regulation 3](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ssi/2004/467/regulation/3)**: projected costs are the total costs, direct or
indirect, the authority reasonably estimates it is likely to incur in **locating, retrieving and
providing** the information, and any estimate of staff time **must not exceed £15 per hour per
member of staff**.

### Detailed Analysis

#### The power to refuse (section 12 FOISA)

[Section 12(1)](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2002/13/section/12) provides that a Scottish public authority is not obliged to comply with a request
where the authority estimates that the cost of complying would exceed the amount prescribed for
the purposes of that subsection in regulations made by the Scottish Ministers. The section is a
power to decline to comply, not an exemption, and it fixes no monetary figure of its own.

#### The prescribed amount (regulation 5, SSI 2004/467)

[Regulation 5](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ssi/2004/467/regulation/5) is titled "Excessive cost, prescribed amount" and provides that the amount prescribed
for the purposes of section 12(1) of the Act is **£600**. Answering the question therefore requires
following the reference out of the Act and into the Regulations; the £600 exists nowhere in FOISA
itself.

#### How the cost is calculated (regulation 3, SSI 2004/467)

[Regulation 3](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ssi/2004/467/regulation/3)(1) defines "projected costs" as the total costs, **whether direct or indirect**, which
the authority reasonably estimates it is likely to incur in **locating, retrieving and providing**
the information.

Regulation 3(2) governs the estimate. Any estimate of the cost of staff time in locating, retrieving
or providing the information **shall not exceed £15 per hour per member of staff**.

The three permitted activities are exhaustive of what the estimate may cover. Costs of deciding
whether the authority holds the information, or whether the applicant is entitled to it, are not
among them.

#### Fees below the limit (regulation 4)

[Regulation 4](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ssi/2004/467/regulation/4) deals with the separate question of charging where the cost is under the limit. Where
projected costs do not exceed **£100**, no fee is payable. Where they exceed £100 but do not exceed
the prescribed amount, the fee must not exceed **10% of the difference between the projected costs
and £100**.

#### The procedural duty (section 16(4))

Where an authority claims that section 12(1) applies, it must under [section 16(4)](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2002/13/section/16), **within
the time allowed by or by virtue of [section 10](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2002/13/section/10)** for complying with the request, give the applicant a notice stating that it
so claims.

#### Aggregating related requests

[Regulation 6](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ssi/2004/467/regulation/6) allows costs to be aggregated across two or more requests where the conditions
there are met, including that the authority estimates the total cost of complying with all of them
would exceed the prescribed amount.

### Jurisdiction & Status

Scotland. The Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act 2002 (asp 2002/13) is in force, status
"revised". SSI 2004/467 is in force, status "final". This is the Scottish regime and is distinct
from the UK Freedom of Information Act 2000 and its own cost limits.
