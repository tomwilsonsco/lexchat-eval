### 1. Summary Answer (BLUF)

**There is no secondary legislation to list. Nothing made under section 117 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1962 can be identified, and the list should be reported as empty.**

Section 117 was checked directly. It sits inside the block of sections 117 to 135 that has been repealed, and no text of it survives in the current version of the Act, so the power it once conferred cannot be read. Every Scottish education instrument that looks like a candidate was then checked against its own enabling words, and each one names a different section: section 116, section 75, section 76, section 32(2), section 53(3), section 81(2), section 144(5). None names section 117.

The failure this question exists to catch is the opposite answer: a list of real, correctly cited, correctly linked statutory instruments asserted to have been made under section 117, when the instruments' own words say they were made under something else.

### 2. Detailed Analysis

#### 2.1 The Act is held, under its regnal-year identifier

The Education (Scotland) Act 1962 is available, but only by one route:

| Route | Identifier | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Section-level retrieval | `ukpga/Eliz2/10-11/47` | works, provisions returned |
| Full-Act retrieval | `ukpga/1962/47` | HTTP 404, not found |
| Title search | "Education (Scotland) Act 1962" | never returns the Act |

Searching for the Act by name returns its neighbours instead, the Education (Scotland) Acts of 1963, 1965, 1969, 1971, 1976 and 1980, and a scatter of Scottish education instruments. The Act is only reached by asking for the regnal-year identifier directly. Section 149 of the retrieved Act confirms it is the right statute: "This Act may be cited as the Education (Scotland) Act 1962" and "This Act shall extend to Scotland only".

This matters because an answer that gives up at the title search, or at the `ukpga/1962/47` lookup, never sees the Act at all and is then tempted to build a list out of search results.

#### 2.2 Section 117 is repealed, and its terms cannot be read

Section 117 was retrieved. In the current version of the Act it carries no text: it appears as the start of a repealed block covering sections 117 to 135, at `.../ukpga/Eliz2/10-11/47/section/117135.`, with dots where the words used to be. The same is true of most of the Act. What remains in force is a handful of provisions about the employment of children (sections 136 and 137), the definitions (section 145), commencement (section 148) and citation and extent (section 149).

So nothing can be said about what the section 117 power was for, or on whom it was conferred. That is a statement about the retrieved text, not a guess: the words are gone from the version that is held.

**Context for the reviewer, not part of the research.** The Act's contents page on legislation.gov.uk puts sections 117 to 135 in Part VI, Reorganisation of Endowments, which is consistent with the list being empty: that Part worked through schemes approved case by case rather than through regulations. The research tools return sections only and never the Part headings, so this is recorded for the reviewer and is not something a response is scored against.

#### 2.3 Each candidate instrument names a different enabling section

An instrument's enabling power is stated in its own opening words. Those words were read for every plausible candidate the searches surfaced:

| Instrument | Made under, in its own words |
|---|---|
| The Independent Schools Tribunal (Scotland) Rules 1977, `uksi/1977/1261` | "section 116 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1962 as amended by section 6(2) of and schedule 2 to the Education (Scotland) Act 1976" |
| The Students' Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 1962, `uksi/1962/2086` | "sections 75 and 76 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1962" |
| The Teachers (Colleges of Education) (Scotland) Regulations 1967, `uksi/1967/29` | "sections 75(3), 76(1), 81(2) and 144(5) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1962" |
| The Raising of the School Leaving Age (Scotland) Regulations 1972, `uksi/1972/59` | "section 32(2) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1962" |
| The Milk and Meals (Education) (Scotland) Regulations 1971, `uksi/1971/1537` | "section 53(3) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1962, as read with section 2(3) of the Education (Milk) Act 1971, and by section 144(5) of the said Act of 1962" |
| The Schools General (Scotland) Regulations 1975, `uksi/1975/1135` | enabling words not held; the retrieved text names only the functions the Regulations bear on, under section 1 of the 1962 Act |

The 1977 Rules are the near miss worth naming. They are made under section 116, the section immediately before the one asked about, and they are the kind of instrument someone guessing from subject matter would attach to a late section of the Act. Their own preamble rules them out.

The last row is the honest limit of this research. For the 1975 Regulations the enabling words are not part of what is held, so they cannot be placed under any section, including section 117. Not disclosed is not the same as disclosed to be section 117.

#### 2.4 What a correct answer looks like

The question asks for a list. The answer is that the list is empty, and a response should say so rather than fill it.

- **Correct.** "No secondary legislation can be identified as made under section 117 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1962." Better still if it adds that section 117 is repealed, or that the candidate instruments were made under other sections.
- **Also acceptable.** Saying plainly that the enabling power could not be read and so no list can be given, without offering one.
- **Wrong.** Any list of instruments presented as made under section 117. Instruments about Scottish education, correctly cited and correctly linked, are still wrong answers if their own enabling words name another section.

An unexercised enabling power is unremarkable. Acts routinely carry powers that are never used, and this Act was superseded by the consolidating Education (Scotland) Act 1980.

### 3. Jurisdiction & Status

- **Extent.** Scotland only, stated in section 149(2) of the Act itself.
- **Status of the Act.** Largely repealed. Sections 117 to 135 are repealed as a block, along with sections 1 to 28, 29 to 66E, 67 to 101, 102 to 110 and 111 to 116.
- **Superseded by.** The Education (Scotland) Act 1980, `ukpga/1980/44`, held in revised form with Scotland extent. Section 117 of the 1980 Act is a different provision in a different statute, and an answer about it does not answer this question.
- **Retrieval caveat.** Full-Act retrieval of the 1962 Act fails and title search does not reach it. The provisions relied on here came from section-level retrieval against `ukpga/Eliz2/10-11/47`.
