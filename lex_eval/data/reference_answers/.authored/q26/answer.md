### Summary Answer (BLUF)

**This answer is not a complete list, and the question asks for one.** What follows are instruments
confirmed from their own text to amend Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013, plus the Scottish framework
that governs how it may be modified. The limits of the list are stated below and are part of the
answer.

Three instruments are confirmed direct amenders, each by its own regulation 2 headed "Amendment of
Regulation (EU) 1306/2013":

- **The Common Agricultural Policy (Financing, Management and Monitoring) (Miscellaneous
  Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (SI 2019/763)**, [regulation 2](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/uksi/2019/763/regulation/2), amending it
  **insofar as it relates to common organisation of the markets and rural development measures**,
  in accordance with regulations 3 to 11.
- **The Financing, Management and Monitoring of Direct Payments to Farmers (Amendment) Regulations
  2020 (SI 2020/90)**, [regulation 2](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/90/regulation/2), amending it
  **in so far as it relates to CAP direct payments**, in accordance with regulations 3 to 11.
- **The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (SI 2019/748)**,
  [regulation 2](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/uksi/2019/748/regulation/2), amending it
  **without any aspect limitation**, in accordance with regulations 3 to 27.

Note the shape of the first two. Each is drafted to bite on one aspect of the Regulation rather than
on the whole of it, which is why an answer that finds one and stops has found half the picture.

### Detailed Analysis

#### How the Regulation applies in Scotland

Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013, the "Horizontal Regulation", was retained in domestic law and is
amended aspect by aspect: SI 2019/763 addresses it as it relates to the common organisation of the
markets and rural development, SI 2020/90 as it relates to CAP direct payments, and SI 2019/748
without any such limitation. The reviewer's understanding is that this reflects two retention
routes, the **Direct Payments to Farmers (Legislative Continuity) Act 2020** for direct payments and
the **European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018** for the rest. Neither Act was retrieved by this
research, so that framing is recorded for the reviewer and is not part of what a response is scored
against; describing the two as parallel mechanisms is an equally reasonable reading.

#### Confirmed direct amendments

**SI 2019/763, [regulation 2](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/uksi/2019/763/regulation/2).** Provides that Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 on the financing,
management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy "is amended, insofar as it relates to
common organisation of the markets and rural development".

**SI 2020/90, [regulation 2](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/90/regulation/2).** Provides that the Regulation "in so far as it relates to the Common
Agricultural Policy ('CAP') direct payments" is amended.

**SI 2019/748, [regulation 2](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/uksi/2019/748/regulation/2).** Provides that Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 "is
amended in accordance with regulations 3 to 27". This is a direct amendment on the face of the
retrieved text: regulation 2 contains no aspect limitation and applies the amendments in
regulations 3 to 27.

#### The Scottish modification framework

The **Agriculture (Retained EU Law and Data) (Scotland) Act 2020 (asp 2020/17)** is the Scottish
vehicle: it [defines the Horizontal Regulation](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2020/17/section/1) and confers power on the Scottish Ministers to
[simplify, improve or update](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2020/17/section/2), [continue or end the operation of](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2020/17/section/3) and
[modify financial provision in](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2020/17/section/4) the retained CAP legislation. It was commenced by SSI 2020/315 (No. 1) and SSI 2020/373 (No. 2). The
**Agriculture and Rural Communities (Scotland) Act 2024 (asp 2024/11)** amends and expands that
framework, restating those powers at [sections 22](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2024/11/section/22), [23](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2024/11/section/23) and [24](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2024/11/section/24) and
[repealing section 5 of the 2020 Act](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/asp/2024/11/section/25).

#### Supplementing and implementing acts

Amendments also reach the Regulation indirectly through its supplementing and implementing acts,
notably **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 907/2014** and **Commission Implementing
Regulation (EU) No 908/2014**, both of which are expressly made under Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013.
An instrument amending those does not amend 1306/2013 itself.

#### What could not be established

The question asks for **every** amending instrument. That could not be established from the
legislation tools available:

1. **Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 itself was not retrievable.** Searching for it returns amending
   instruments, not the Regulation, so its amendment history could not be read off the consolidated
   text.
2. **There is no way to search by "what amends X".** The tools search by title and by text within a
   named instrument, so an exhaustive list would require reading every candidate instrument in turn.

A correct answer should therefore either give a list and say plainly that it is not verified as
complete, or say that completeness could not be established. Presenting a partial list as exhaustive
is wrong. A response naming amending instruments beyond the three confirmed here is going further
than this answer, not contradicting it.

### Jurisdiction & Status

Scotland, within a largely UK-wide instrument set. SI 2019/763, SI 2020/90 and SI 2019/748 are
UK-extent and in force, status "revised". The Agriculture (Retained EU Law and Data) (Scotland) Act
2020 and the Agriculture and Rural Communities (Scotland) Act 2024 extend to Scotland and are in
force, status "revised".
