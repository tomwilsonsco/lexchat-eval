## 1. Question

> Provide a list of all secondary legislation made under the enabling powers in section 117 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1962

Legislation only. Case law is out of scope, so an answer that turns on a decided case is not expected to cite one here.

**Scope of this answer:** Covers whether any secondary legislation was made under the enabling power in section 117 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1962, and whether that Act can be retrieved at all. Limited to legislation. Does not cover section 117 of the later Education (Scotland) Act 1980.

### 1. Summary Answer (BLUF)

**The list is empty. No secondary legislation has ever been made under section 117 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1962.**

That is the whole answer, and it is the answer a correct response must reach. The failure this question exists to catch is the opposite: producing a list of real, correctly cited, correctly linked statutory instruments and asserting that each was made under section 117, when none of them was.

A second, compounding problem sits underneath it. **The Education (Scotland) Act 1962 could not be retrieved at all** from the available legislation sources. Section 117 was never read. An answer that lists instruments made under a provision it could not open is not a partial answer, it is an invented one.

### 2. Detailed Analysis

#### 2.1 The 1962 Act is not available to be read

Two direct attempts to retrieve the Act by identifier both returned **HTTP 404**:

| Attempt | Identifier | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Modern-form citation | `ukpga/1962/47` | HTTP 404, not found |
| Regnal-year citation | `ukpga/Eliz2/10-11/47` | HTTP 404, not found |

Four searches were also run. None returned the Education (Scotland) Act 1962. What they returned instead were the neighbouring Education (Scotland) Acts that **are** held, namely the Acts of 1963 (`ukpga/1963/21`), 1965 (`ukpga/1965/7`), 1969 (`ukpga/1969/49`), 1971 (`ukpga/1971/42`) and 1980 (`ukpga/1980/44`), together with unrelated instruments.

This matters for how the absence should be read. The corpus plainly holds Scottish education legislation from the same era, so the 1962 Act's absence is a specific gap rather than a general one. Either way, **section 117 was not retrieved, and nothing about its terms can be asserted**.

#### 2.2 What the searches did return, and why none of it answers the question

The searches surfaced instruments that are genuine, correctly titled and plausibly education-related:

- The Schools General (Scotland) Regulations 1975 (`uksi/1975/1135`)
- The Students' Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 1962 (`uksi/1962/2086`)
- The Education Authority Bursaries (Scotland) (Amendment No. 3) Provisional Regulations 1962 (`uksi/1962/1294`)
- The Milk and Meals (Education) (Scotland) Regulations 1971 (`uksi/1971/1537`)
- The Raising of the School Leaving Age (Scotland) Regulations 1972 (`uksi/1972/59`)
- The Independent Schools Tribunal (Scotland) Rules 1977 (`uksi/1977/1261`)

Every one of these is a real instrument. **None of them was retrieved with any evidence of the power it was made under.** A search result records an instrument's identifier, title, year, status and extent. It does not record the enabling provision. Nothing in the retrieved material connects any of these instruments to section 117, and their subject matter (bursaries, allowances, school meals, leaving age, tribunal rules) is not evidence of the enabling power either.

Listing any of them as "made under section 117" would be asserting a fact that was never retrieved. That is precisely the recorded failure mode for this question.

#### 2.3 Why the correct answer is "none"

The reviewer's position, and the answer against which a response should be measured, is that **no secondary legislation has ever been made under section 117 of the 1962 Act**. An enabling power that is never exercised is unremarkable: Acts routinely carry powers that are never used, and the 1962 Act was in any event overtaken by the consolidating Education (Scotland) Act 1980.

So there are two acceptable shapes for a correct response, and one unacceptable one:

- **Correct.** "No secondary legislation has been made under section 117 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1962; the list is empty."
- **Also acceptable.** "I could not retrieve the Education (Scotland) Act 1962 or its section 117, so I cannot confirm what was made under it," said plainly and without a list.
- **Wrong.** Any list of instruments presented as having been made under section 117.

### 3. Jurisdiction & Status

- **Extent.** Scotland. The Education (Scotland) Act 1962 was a Scotland-only Act.
- **Superseded.** The 1962 Act was overtaken by the Education (Scotland) Act 1980 (`ukpga/1980/44`), which is held in revised form with Scotland extent. Care is needed not to answer about section 117 of the 1980 Act instead: the question asks about the 1962 Act, and the two are different statutes.
- **No sources retrieved.** Unusually for a reference answer, this one rests on retrieval that returned no provision text at all. That is the finding, not a defect in the research: both identifier lookups returned 404 and no search reached the Act.
- **Not addressed.** The terms of section 117, its subject matter and its scope are not stated here, because the provision could not be read.
