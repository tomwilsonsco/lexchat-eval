## 2. Answer

# Section 6, Data Protection Act 2018 — Meaning of "controller"

## 1. Summary Answer (BLUF)

Section 6 of the Data Protection Act 2018 (c. 12) is a short interpretive provision headed **"Meaning of 'controller'"**. It does **not** create a free-standing definition; instead it qualifies the definition of "controller" in Article 4(1)(7) of the UK GDPR, making it subject to subsection (2), section 209 and section 210, and it provides that where personal data is processed **only** for purposes, and by means, *required by an enactment*, the person on whom that statutory obligation falls is the controller. It sits in **Part 2 (general processing), Chapter 2** of the Act (Chapter 2 now headed "The UK GDPR"), and is in force as amended by the EU Exit Regulations 2019 and, in consequence, the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.

---

## 2. Detailed Analysis

### 2.1 Full text of section 6 (as retrieved in current revised form)

> **Section 6 — Meaning of "controller"**
>
> (1) The definition of "controller" in Article 4(1)(7) of the UK GDPR has effect subject to—
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) subsection (2),
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) section 209, and
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) section 210.
>
> (2) For the purposes of the UK GDPR, where personal data is processed only—
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) for purposes for which it is required by an enactment to be processed, and
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) by means by which it is required by an enactment to be processed,
> the person on whom the obligation to process the data is imposed by the enactment (or, if different, one of the enactments) is the controller.

([Data Protection Act 2018, s.6](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ukpga/2018/12/section/6))

### 2.2 Effect

**(a) A modifier, not a definition.** Section 6 operates only on the UK GDPR's own definition of "controller" (Art. 4(1)(7)). This reciprocal relationship is confirmed on the UK GDPR side: the EU Exit Regulations amended Article 4(7) so that, after "determined", the words referring to Union/Member State law were replaced with "**(but see section 6 of the 2018 Act)**" ([SI 2019/419, Sch. 1, para. 6(3)](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/uksi/2019/419/schedule/1)).

**(b) The statutory-duty rule (s.6(2)).** Where *both* the purposes and the means of processing are mandated by an enactment, controllership is allocated by statute to the person bearing that obligation, displacing the ordinary "determines the purposes and means" test. This is a drafting pattern repeated across the Act:
- **Part 3 (law enforcement processing):** s.32(1)–(2) — "controller" means the *competent authority* which determines purposes and means, or which is the controller by virtue of the equivalent enactment rule ([s.32](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ukpga/2018/12/section/32)).
- **Part 4 (intelligence services processing):** s.83(1)–(2), in materially identical terms ([s.83](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ukpga/2018/12/section/83)).

**(c) Section 6 is subject to ss.209 and 210.** Subsection (1)(b)–(c) subordinates the definition to those two sections. Note: the retrieval did not return the text of ss.209–210, and the available database extract therefore does not confirm their subject-matter beyond the fact that both were amended by SI 2019/419 (Sch. 2, paras. 88–89) to substitute "UK GDPR" for "GDPR" in s.209(2)–(4) and s.210(2)–(3). **The available database does not contain, in the material retrieved, the operative text of sections 209 and 210.**

### 2.3 Definitions established / interaction with other definitions

Section 6 is one of four controllership provisions signposted by the Act's central definitions section:

> s.3(6): "'Controller' and 'processor', in relation to the processing of personal data to which … Part 2, Part 3 or Part 4 applies, have the same meaning as in that … Part (**see sections 5, 6, 32 and 83** and see also subsection (14)(d))."
> ([s.3](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ukpga/2018/12/section/3))

Related definitional architecture:
- **s.5 ("Definitions")** — terms used in Part 2 and in the UK GDPR bear their UK GDPR meaning, read with any Part 2 provision modifying that meaning, and subject to any express contrary provision and s.204 ([s.5](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ukpga/2018/12/section/5)). Section 6 is precisely such a modifying provision.
- **s.3(10)** — "the UK GDPR" means Regulation (EU) 2016/679 as it forms part of domestic law by virtue of s.3 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 ([s.3](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ukpga/2018/12/section/3)).
- **s.206** — index of defined expressions (s.3(15)).
- Note s.6 defines *only* "controller"; there is no "processor" limb in Part 2 (contrast s.32(3) and s.83(3), which each define "processor" for Parts 3 and 4 respectively).

### 2.4 Place within the structure of the Act

| Level | Location |
|---|---|
| Part | **Part 2 — General processing** |
| Chapter | **Chapter 2** — originally "The GDPR", now headed "**The UK GDPR**" |
| Cross-heading | Italic heading immediately before s.6, originally "The GDPR", now "**The UK GDPR**" |
| Neighbouring sections | s.4 (processing to which Part applies), s.5 (definitions), s.6, s.7 (public authorities/bodies), s.8 (lawfulness: public interest) |

The structural evidence comes from the amending instrument: SI 2019/419, Sch. 2, para. 5 is headed "**Part 2 (general processing)**" and, within it, para. 7 directs "For the heading of **Chapter 2** substitute 'The UK GDPR'"; para. 8 directs "In the **italic heading before section 6**, for 'GDPR' substitute 'UK GDPR'"; para. 9 then amends s.6 itself ([SI 2019/419, Sch. 2](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/uksi/2019/419/schedule/2)).

Note also that Chapter 3 of Part 2 (the "applied GDPR" regime, ss.21–28) was substantially dismantled by the same Regulations, and Schedule 6 (which had applied the GDPR to non-EU-law processing) was omitted (SI 2019/419, Sch. 2, paras. 27–36, 96; and see [Sch. 6 DPA 2018](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ukpga/2018/12/schedule/6), now shown as repealed).

### 2.5 Amendments since enactment

**(1) SI 2019/419 — the EU Exit Regulations (in force IP completion day)**

- **Schedule 2, para. 9:** "In **section 6(1) and (2)**, for 'GDPR' substitute '**UK GDPR**'."
- **Schedule 2, para. 8:** italic heading before s.6 amended in the same way.
([The Data Protection, Privacy and Electronic Communications (Amendments etc) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, Sch. 2](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/uksi/2019/419/schedule/2))

Commencement of those amendments: "Subject to paragraph (3), they come into force on exit day" ([SI 2019/419, reg. 1](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/uksi/2019/419/regulation/1)). Regulation 4 is the operative provision ("Schedule 2 amends the 2018 Act") ([reg. 4](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/uksi/2019/419/regulation/4)).

**(2) Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, s.67(2) — consequential renumbering**

> s.67(2): "In consequence of the amendment made by subsection (1)(a), **in section 6 of the 2018 Act (meaning of 'controller'), for '4(7)' substitute '4(1)(7)'**."
([Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, s.67](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ukpga/2025/18/section/67))

This is purely consequential on s.67(1)(a) restructuring Article 4 of the UK GDPR (the existing text becoming Article 4(1), with new paragraphs (2)–(5) on research and statistical purposes). The revised text of s.6 retrieved from the database already reads "Article 4(1)(7)".

**(3) Commencement status of the 2025 amendment.** Section 67 is **not** among the provisions brought into force on passing or after two months by s.142(2)–(4) of the 2025 Act; it therefore "comes into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by regulations appoint" ([DUAA 2025, s.142](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ukpga/2025/18/section/142)). The available database does not record the commencement regulations for s.67, so I cannot confirm from the API whether the "4(1)(7)" substitution is fully commenced or is displayed as a prospective/revised amendment.

**(4) Prospective amendments.** No further amendment to s.6 itself was located. However, two prospective changes to the wider Part 1/Part 2 definitional framework in which s.6 operates are recorded:
- **s.3(8A)** inserting "the Commission" (Information Commission) — DUAA 2025, s.117 ([s.117](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ukpga/2025/18/section/117)) — and the abolition of the office of Information Commissioner with consequential omission of s.3(8) ([s.118](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ukpga/2025/18/section/118)).
- Amendments to s.83 (Part 4 controllership) inserting a new subsection (A1) covering intelligence services and qualifying competent authorities under designation notices ([DUAA 2025, s.90](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/id/ukpga/2025/18/section/90)).

These do not alter the text of s.6 but affect the surrounding definitional context.
